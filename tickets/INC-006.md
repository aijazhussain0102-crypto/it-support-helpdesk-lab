## 1. Issue Description
Workstation responsiveness degraded; CPU pinned at 100% and disk activity maxed out.

## 2. Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
Low disk space (< 5% free on C:) caused Windows Update TiWorker.exe to enter continuous retry loop.

## 3. Resolution Steps
Purged SoftwareDistribution download cache, ran cleanmgr, freed 12 GB, and rebooted after update completed.

## 4. Verification & Prevention
- **Verification:** Free space restored to 28 GB; CPU dropped to 3% idle baseline.
- **Prevention:** Enforce standardized Group Policy baselines, automated monitoring scripts, and least-privilege role separation.
